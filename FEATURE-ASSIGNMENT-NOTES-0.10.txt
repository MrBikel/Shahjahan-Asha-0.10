Shahjahan Asha 0.10 private-test feature assignments

Reference: Microsoft, Developing OpenType Fonts for Bengali
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/script-development/bengali

Microsoft lists Bengali basic-shaping order as nukt, akhn, rphf, blwf, half, pstf, vatu, cjct; presentation features follow, and GPOS follows GSUB.
The existing 0.9 ক্ষ and ক্ষ্ম candidates remain in akhn as explicitly retained experiments.
The new ক্ক, ক্ত and ক্ল direct conjunct substitutions are assigned to cjct; this partial font has no half-form feature or half-form glyphs. The guide describes cjct as the conjunct path particularly needed when a pre-base consonant has no half form.
ক্র is assigned to vatu: the guide specifically describes vatu as a consonant plus below-base vattu (Bengali ra-phala) ligature and illustrates Ka + below-base Ra.
These are narrowly scoped tests, not complete Bengali shaping. The font does not implement the other basic GSUB or presentation-form features.
